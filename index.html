How to play

Playing is as easy as 1-2-3. You'll be in the game before anyone finishes reading the box.

1

Load it up

Clench a Shart token between your (clothed!) cheeks.

2

Take aim

Waddle over to the toilet target and line up your shot.

3

Fire in the hole

Loosen up and release. Land it in the bowl to score.

3 ways to play

Classic Shartz

First to drain the shot wins

  • Form a line to set who shoots in what order.
  • One at a time, try to land it in the target.
  • Miss, and you go to the back of the line.
  • Make it, and you score a point for that round. First to 3 points wins!

Rotten Egg

Last player to score loses

  • Same as Classic, but once you make a shot, you're safe and done shooting.
  • Miss, and you get back in line to shoot again.
  • Don't be the last one to make it!

Plop-a-Shot

60 seconds to score as many points as possible

  • Start a timer and make as many shots as you can.
  • Most points wins!
  • Works best with 2 toilets.
